DoS Ingress Filtering

Enforce rate limiting on TCP SYN packets across perimeter load balancers. Configure TCP SYN cookies (net.ipv4.tcp_syncookies = 1) to mitigate resource starvation.

Portsweep & Probe Mitigation

Drop incoming probe scans with dynamic firewall threshold rules. Mask inactive port responses using silent packet drops rather than explicit TCP RST replies.

Credential & R2L Hardening
